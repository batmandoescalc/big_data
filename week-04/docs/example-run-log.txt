READING THESE NUMBERS
The local banding step takes 0.4 s; the same work on Hadoop takes about 60 s.
On 2.2 MB of input, Hadoop's time goes to starting JVMs, asking YARN for
containers, scheduling, and shuffling, not to banding: the banding job used
only 11 s of CPU across all tasks. Two runs of identical jobs differed by 12 to
20 s per job. These timings show Hadoop's fixed overhead on small data. They
say nothing about how either approach scales.
